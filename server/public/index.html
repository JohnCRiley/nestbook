Which Plan Is Right for You?

Free Forever

Perfect if you run a B&B or guesthouse with up to 5 rooms, or a single villa, lodge, gîte or holiday cottage — and just need a simple booking webpage with photos, a booking form, and basic booking, room and guest management.

Pro

Perfect if you own one property that fits any of these:

  • Individual rooms — B&Bs, guesthouses, boutique hotels, inns with rooms
  • A single whole-property rental — villas, lodges, holiday cottages, individual holiday apartments, gîtes, casas rurales, chalets
  • A single self-catering site with multiple units — aparthotels, holiday rental apartment blocks, glamping sites, small caravan parks
Multi

Perfect if you own a mix of up to 5 properties included in the Pro plan.

Every plan includes a full booking, room and guest management suite, your own booking webpage, automatic email confirmations, and card payments via Stripe.
